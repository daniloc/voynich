**Status legend**
- 🟢 **SURVIVED** — passed at least one genuine adversarial control (null model, confound partial-out, or held-out validation).
- 🔴 **KILLED** — was asserted with confidence, then demolished by a later control (often our own).
- 🟡 **LIVE** — testable, partially tested, or untested; the residual where meaning could still hide.
- ⚪ **EXTERNAL** — corroborated by outside scholarship, not just our pipeline.

## 3. The recurring confounds (the killers — check every new claim against these)

1. **Currier A/B dialect leakage** — `edy/oke/qok` (B) vs `ain/cha/hai` (A) separate groups that look like they split on something else. Always check dialect balance of any contrast.
2. **Production-unit / dialect-block autocorrelation** (*refined 2026-06-20, `quire_test.py`, L7*) — pages made together share vocabulary, but the structure is **discrete block membership, NOT smooth shelf-distance**. Within the herbal (topic held constant): similarity is driven by **same-Currier (raw r +0.52) ≫ same-quire / same-hand**, while **smooth reading-distance is ~0 once blocks are controlled (partial r = −0.012)**; there's a real quire-boundary *step* (+0.059 at matched distance) a distance control can't see. **So partial out quire+hand+Currier block identity, not folio distance** — distance-only controls were leaky (under-correct within-quire, over-correct across boundaries). Corollary: Fagin-Davis bifolio *reordering* would change little, because the unit is the gathering/dialect block, not fine reading position.
3. **Content-free reproducibility** — the repository's hard-coded schema/copy generator misses raw directional morphotactics, while a fitted first-order word-Markov generator reproduces the qualitative arrow. Neither is globally matched: the former inherits unrefitted copy parameters and the latter is fitted to the observed transition table. Call a null "matched" only on quantities it actually matches.
4. **English-as-floor** — never use English alone as the "real language" baseline; it's an outlier (deep orthography). Use templatic/CV controls (Hawaiian, Māori, Latin, Turkish).
5. **Local copying mimics order** — lag-decay and adjacent near-variants come free from copying; refit the copy knob to real burstiness before claiming "order information."
6. **Deterministic coarsening masquerades as new information** — a word's prefix and suffix are functions of that word. Once exact-word identity is in the model, a morph class can only improve finite-sample backoff; it cannot carry independent population information. Require a positive held-out nested likelihood gain before treating a coarsened feature as a solver clue.
