Five Sonnet analysts read all seven prior session transcripts end-to-end (plus the ~113
subagent reports embedded in them), each hunting for unturned stones: ideas raised but

  collision census anti-referential (within-ring duplicate excess = copying signature).

  prefix-carried signal at pharma n=35, never a core-carried one; the balneo arm was
  never statistically run (eyeballed), though the raw 8-agent JSON has real variation
  (incl. label-binds-to-nymph vs -object). Desk-runnable from existing data.

  Italian vernacular target was ever tried, despite M9's transalpine exemplar finding.
  "Major cipher families excluded" is broader than what was tested. Cheap re-run.

  independently converged on the same design (anchor ~200 picture-cribs from

  decisive falsifier; R1 ran a weaker unanchored version.
- **K1/K10 metric swap.** `topic_gen.py`'s pro-content result (burstiness B=0.138 and
  hapax 0.70 unreachable by its generators — once called "the strongest result of the
  entire investigation") was superseded by `residual.py` scored on a *different*
  statistic (IoD). Never reconciled on the same metric. One re-run seals it.
- **Named rivals never confronted:** the scott-schechter Latin-Occitan glossary, the
