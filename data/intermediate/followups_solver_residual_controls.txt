LIMITATIONS
  1. Prefix pooling is representational backoff, not additional information beyond exact word in the population.
  2. Latin, English, Naibbe, and generated controls use artificial Voynich line/quire blocks; source sentence and page boundaries are lost.
  3. Schema tables are estimated globally from Voynich and generator hyperparameters are inherited, not nested or refitted.
  4. The one-replicate procedural arms are deterministic calibration points, not empirical procedural-null distributions.
  5. Accuracy is secondary; proper held-out log score is primary. The two-character analysis was declared as a sensitivity, not selected.
